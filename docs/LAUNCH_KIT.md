## Positioning

**Name:** LaunchScope Open  
**Tagline (under 60 characters):** Free, open-source website launch readiness checker

**One-line pitch:** Check 12 website-launch essentials, get a readable score and export a report, with no signup and no paid API.

**Who it's for:** Independent developers, web designers, freelancers, website owners and small teams preparing to launch a public website.

**What it checks:** Basic HTTPS, reachability, title and description, H1, mobile viewport, favicon, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and selected security headers.

**Boundaries:** This isn't a performance benchmark, accessibility audit, penetration test, deep SEO review, or a guarantee of rankings or security. The free Render demo might sleep while inactive.
